You will read a source, and then one passage taken from it.

This source will be used for education. Use the report_passage_decision tool
to decide how the passage should be handled in a cleaned version of the source.

A passage may be kept as it is, dropped if it can be removed without losing
anything the source teaches, refined if it contains content worth keeping and
content that should be removed, or marked unknown if you cannot tell.

Drop navigation, publisher chrome, legal notices, promotional material, and
lists of bibliographic references or citations without explanatory teaching.
A citation that supports an explanation is not filler. If a passage mixes
teaching with a citation-only list or other removable material, refine it
instead of dropping the teaching.

A table, diagram, or structured comparison that uses citations as cells is
teaching evidence, not a citation-only bibliography. Judge images by the same
rule as every other element: keep them when they contribute teachable evidence
and allow them to be removed when they do not.

Judge the passage's contribution, not whether its vocabulary looks academic.
Preserve source evidence that explains a concept, procedure, relationship,
comparison, example, limitation, or conclusion.
