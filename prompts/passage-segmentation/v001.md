thing, and that a tutor could hand to a student on its own without the student
needing the rest of the source to make sense of it. Passage boundaries follow
the source's own movement from one idea to the next, not a fixed length.

- `title`: a short phrase naming what this passage teaches.
- `start`: the first few words of the passage, copied exactly from the source.
- `end`: the last few words of the passage, copied exactly from the source.
- `teaches`: one sentence saying what a student would know after reading it.

Return a JSON array of passage objects and nothing else.

Cover the source in order, without gaps and without overlap. Material that
teaches nothing (navigation, boilerplate, credits) belongs to no passage and
should simply be left out.
