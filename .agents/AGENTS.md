# CG Pipeline pilot

CG Pipeline is the Source Publication pilot that turns institutional Syllabi and
their Sources into auditable publications, reviewed Lesson Builds, immutable
Graph Revisions, and validated Companion Packages.

## Branching

For every pilot ticket, branch from `pilot` and merge back into `pilot`. Do not
use `main` for pilot work.
